Based on my comprehensive analysis of your recipe API, here are the key interconnected issues and specific improvements needed to match popular recipe sites like AllRecipes, Food Network, or Epicurious:

🔴 Critical Issues Found
1. Recipe Content Structure is Too Basic
Current: Only a single description field for the entire recipe
Problem: No structured steps, timers, or ingredient references
Popular Sites: Numbered steps with optional timers, notes, and ingredient highlighting
2. User-Scoped Tags/Ingredients Break Discoverability
Current: Tags and ingredients are user-owned, but recipes are globally viewable
Problem: User A's "garlic" tag isn't visible to User B, even though both have garlic recipes
Popular Sites: Global taxonomy with user tagging/personalization
3. No Recipe Status Control
Current: All recipes are immediately visible to authenticated users
Problem: No drafts, private recipes, or publishing workflow
Popular Sites: Draft/published/private states with granular sharing
4. Missing Essential Recipe Metadata
Current: Only total time_minutes and basic fields
Problem: No prep vs cook time, servings, difficulty, cuisine, dietary restrictions
Popular Sites: Rich metadata for filtering and meal planning
🟡 Authentication & Permission Issues
5. Limited Authentication Options
Current: Only TokenAuthentication
Problem: Poor mobile experience, no social logins, no JWT
Popular Sites: Multiple auth methods (email, Google, Facebook, Apple)
6. Overly Permissive Recipe Access
Current: Any authenticated user can view all recipes
Problem: No concept of public vs private recipes
Popular Sites: Granular privacy controls (public, followers-only, private)
🟢 Missing Social & Discovery Features
7. No Social Features
Current: Basic ratings only
Problem: No following users, recipe sharing, bookmarks, collections
Popular Sites: Social feeds, following, recipe collections, sharing
8. Limited Reviews System
Current: Only numeric 1-5 ratings
Problem: No text reviews, comments, or review history
Popular Sites: Rich reviews with photos, comments, and ratings
9. Basic Search & Filtering
Current: Simple tag/ingredient/rating filters
Problem: No full-text search, fuzzy matching, or advanced queries
Popular Sites: Powerful search with auto-complete, filters, and recommendations
